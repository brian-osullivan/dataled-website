## Contact

Brian started his career with tech upstart Google back in 2005. He worked there for 8 years across ads, measurement, and analytics.

He spent 2 years as a senior manager on the growth team at Dropbox, led marketing and was head of growth at 2 Series A SaaS companies and has consulted for several more from Seed to Series C.

He has spoken about growth at events in Dublin, Edinburgh, Brussels, Stockholm, London, Amsterdam, and Moscow

He lives in Ireland with his wife and 2 kids. In his (limited) spare time he likes to get outdoors: surfing, biking (mountain and road), triathlon, and hiking.

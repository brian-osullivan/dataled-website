Many SaaS & lead-gen companies have capable in-house teams but need strategic guidance on where to focus their growth efforts. I work with companies that prefer to handle execution themselves but want an experienced perspective on growth strategy and priorities.

## How I work

I provide strategic advice and guidance rather than hands-on execution. My role is to help you make better decisions about where to invest your time and resources for maximum growth impact.

**Typical engagement:** One strategic session per week, with ongoing access via Slack for quick questions and guidance between meetings.

**Who I work with:** Companies ranging from well-funded startups to established businesses with existing growth teams who want strategic direction rather than additional execution capacity.

## Areas where I provide strategic guidance

### Growth Strategy & Planning

**Growth model design:** Help you understand and optimise your specific growth model, whether that's product-led growth, sales-led, or hybrid approaches.

**Channel strategy:** Strategic guidance on which acquisition channels to prioritise based on your market, product, and stage of growth.

**Metric frameworks:** Establish the right metrics and goals that actually drive your business forward, not just vanity metrics.

**Growth team structure:** Advice on how to organise your growth function and what roles you need as you scale.

### Research & Analysis Projects

**Growth audits:** Comprehensive analysis of your current growth performance to identify the biggest opportunities and bottlenecks.

**User research strategy:** Guidance on what research to conduct to better understand your customers and their journey.

**Data strategy:** Strategic advice on what to measure, how to structure your data, and what insights to prioritise.

### Ongoing Strategic Support

**Weekly strategy sessions:** Regular sessions to discuss current challenges, review performance, and plan next steps.

**Campaign and experiment planning:** Strategic input on your testing roadmap and growth experiments.

**Team mentoring:** Support for your growth team members as they tackle specific challenges.

**Decision support:** Help thinking through important growth-related decisions and trade-offs.

- Guidance based on experience from high-growth SaaS companies

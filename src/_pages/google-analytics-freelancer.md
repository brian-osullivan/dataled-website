Most SaaS and lead-generation companies are flying blind when it comes to understanding how their website and funnels actually perform. I help you implement proper analytics that give you the insights you need to grow your business.

## What I help you measure

**Website analytics:** Understand how visitors interact with your site, where they drop off, and which channels drive your best customers.

**Conversion tracking:** Track the complete customer journey from first visit to paying customer, including complex B2B sales cycles.

**Product analytics:** Measure user behaviour within your application to understand activation, engagement, and retention patterns.

**Marketing performance:** Connect your marketing spend to actual revenue outcomes across all channels.

## Why proper analytics matter

You can't optimise what you can't measure. Without reliable data, you're making decisions based on gut feeling rather than evidence. This leads to wasted marketing budgets, missed opportunities, and slow growth.

Most companies have analytics installed, but they're not capturing the data that actually matters for their business. Generic implementations miss the nuances of B2B sales cycles, product-led growth models, and complex customer journeys.

## Common analytics mistakes I see

**Basic pageview tracking only:** Most companies just add the standard tracking code and leave it there. You're only measuring pageviews, not business outcomes.

**Setup done by non-specialists:** An in-house developer or agency sets up analytics to "get it done" rather than understanding what data you actually need for your business model.

**They think they don't need it:** Some companies view analytics as an expense rather than an investment. Without quality data, your marketing team will struggle to improve performance.

**Using the wrong tools:** Not all analytics platforms are suitable for every business. B2B SaaS companies have different needs than e-commerce sites.
